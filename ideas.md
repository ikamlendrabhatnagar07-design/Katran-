# Katran Design Brief

## Explorations

### 1. The Reuse Atelier — **chosen**
A warm, editorial workshop where fashion feels handmade, capable, and quietly aspirational. The interface contrasts deep ink backgrounds with papery ivory panels, lacquer-red accents, and a vivid acidic green used sparingly for action and impact cues. **Probability: 0.08**

### 2. Material Ledger
A rigorous industrial-circular system inspired by library cards, dye lots, garment tags, and utility stamps. Dense inventory patterns, slate neutrals, narrow sans serif labels, and a restrained operational aesthetic. **Probability: 0.06**

### 3. Folk Future Market
A joyful, hand-painted neighborhood bazaar with sun-faded primary colors, visible brush texture, eclectic labels, and lively textile motifs. **Probability: 0.04**

## Chosen direction: The Reuse Atelier

### Design movement
A contemporary circular-fashion editorial designed as a working studio rather than a conventional thrift marketplace. It makes material transformation feel both precise and personal.

### Core principles
- **Material first:** fabric, silhouettes, and print texture are the visual stars.
- **Tactile clarity:** rounded paper-like panels, stitch-line borders, generous whitespace, and straightforward tools make the product feel approachable.
- **Editorial confidence:** large serif headlines create an aspirational fashion voice; compact sans-serif utility text keeps the maker workflow legible.
- **Visible transformation:** before/after narrative and the live customizer make reuse feel tangible.

### Color philosophy
- Ink `#172820`: calm, deep green-black base for high contrast.
- Paper `#F4F0E7`: warm undyed fabric background.
- Clay `#D95735`: expressive remaking, warmth, and primary calls to action.
- Lime `#C7F000`: optimistic circularity and measurement/status cues.
- Dusty lilac `#DCD4E8`: creative tool and secondary-surface contrast.
- Brass `#C59D5F`: selective craft detail.

### Layout paradigm
A vertically paced editorial landing page. The first fold establishes the mission and acts as an entry point to the studio. The central feature is a split-panel customizer with its controls deliberately exposed—not hidden behind a modal. Follow it with a clear circular process, an editorial marketplace grid, learning opportunities, and an impact close.

### Signature elements
- A looped-ribbon **K** mark evoking a folded garment and circular material flow.
- Raw-edged, rounded content panels with hairline stitch-like borders.
- Offset label chips and small numbered circular markers.
- A dress silhouette that behaves like a canvas: pattern placement sits inside the garment clip path and responds in real time.

### Interaction philosophy
Interactions should be direct and forgiving. Every customizer selection updates instantly. Placement happens by clicking the garment itself; keyboard-accessible buttons provide predictable selection states and the panel maintains clear textual feedback. Motion is subtle: panels lift slightly, marks rotate minimally, and pattern changes use a brief dissolve.

### Animation
Use short, reduced-motion-safe transitions for hover, selection, and scrolling cues. No essential content relies on animation.

### Typography system
- Display: a high-contrast editorial serif stack (`Georgia`, `Times New Roman`, serif).
- Interface: a geometric system sans (`Inter`, `Arial`, sans-serif).
- Labels: uppercase sans with generous letter spacing.

### Brand essence and voice
**Katran** is resourceful, artful, and optimistic. It speaks like an experienced maker who invites newcomers to participate: clear, capable, and never preachy.

### Wordmark and logo
A wordmark in high-contrast serif lettering paired with a simple looped **K** glyph. The glyph is a strong two-curve silhouette in ink and lime, communicating cloth looping back into use. The favicon is an opaque square in ink with the lime glyph centered in generous breathing room.

### Signature brand color
**Katran Lime** (`#C7F000`)—used as a deliberate spark against dark ink, never as a background for long text.

### Asset plan
- **Hero visual:** a fashion-editorial image of a maker’s hands transforming a reclaimed black dress in an ink-and-clay studio, composed with negative space for left-side copy; no text in the image.
- **Custom dress visual:** built in the interface as a vector canvas to preserve control, color changes, pattern scaling, and click-to-place behavior.
- **Marketplace imagery:** bespoke CSS-patterned material swatches and product compositions, avoiding unrelated decorative stock imagery.
