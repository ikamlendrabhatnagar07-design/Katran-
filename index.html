THE KATRAN LOOP

Every piece finds its people.

  1. 01

    Quality check

    We note colour, size, feel, and every flaw worth working with.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Clothes and leftover cuts are picked up before they become waste.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Materials meet tailors, students, and small makers who can use them.

  4. 04

    Distribute

    The repaired, remade, and resold pieces return to real wardrobes.
